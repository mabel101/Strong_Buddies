What we do

We keep childhood going

Together, we support the psychosocial wellbeing and healthy development of children living with chronic and long-term health conditions. We believe that when a child becomes ill, their childhood should not become defined entirely by their illness.

In hospital wards and beyond, we bring four things every child needs.

Stories

Story circles and reading buddies bring books to life at the bedside.

Play

Games, laughter and choice, because play is how children heal and grow.

Art

Painting, drawing and making something that is entirely theirs.

Friends

Someone glad to see them, and friendships that make a child feel they belong.
