Home Learning & Creative Arts Packages

Learning and creativity, delivered to your door.

Not every child can come to a club or a hospital session. Treatment, tiredness, mobility, distance and transport can all get in the way. Our Home Learning and Creativity Packages bring learning, play and creativity to children at home, or during hospital stays where appropriate. They also help us stay connected with registered families between visits and sessions.

What a package may contain

  • Learning materials: storybooks, reading cards, word and alphabet activities, writing exercises, maths games, worksheets and simple guides, aligned with school content where possible and designed to support school, not replace it.
  • Arts and crafts: crayons, pencils, colouring sheets, paper, child-safe glue, craft materials and guided creative projects.
  • Play and discovery: puzzles, activity cards, simple games, matching exercises and storytelling prompts that children can enjoy alone or with family.
  • Activity guides: simple instructions for children and caregivers, including ideas for low-energy days.
