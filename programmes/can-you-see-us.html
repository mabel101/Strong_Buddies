Can You See Us?

A digital media campaign making visible the young people behind the diagnoses.

Volunteer with us Partner with us

What happens

We share the stories of young people living with chronic illness, in their own words, to raise awareness and challenge stigma.

Who it is for

Young people living with chronic illness, and the communities, schools and workplaces around them.

Our aims

  • Raise awareness of what living with chronic illness is like
  • Challenge stigma and misunderstanding
  • Give young people a voice and a platform, with consent and care
  • Help the public see the person, not only the condition
