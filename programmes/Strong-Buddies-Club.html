Strong Buddies Club

The Strong Buddies Club is a weekly, welcoming, non-clinical space for children and adolescents living with chronic illness to learn, play, create and make friends, outside the hospital. It is designed around children whose health may interrupt school, physical activity and ordinary social life. Here, they take part as children first, not as their diagnoses.

What Children Experience

  • Reading and learning. Shared reading, storytelling, reading games, writing, puzzles and age-appropriate numeracy, adapted to each child's age, ability and school level. Children can also revisit what they missed while away from school.
  • Creative arts. Drawing, painting, crafts, storytelling and music, with the focus on imagination and enjoyment, not performance or competition.
  • Play and discovery. Building activities, board games, cooperative challenges and imaginative play. Children can choose quieter options or rest whenever they need to.
  • Friendship. Group activities that help children cooperate, share ideas and build friendships with peers who understand interrupted routines, without having to talk about their illness.

Restoring the child behind the diagnosis.
