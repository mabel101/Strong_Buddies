Alongside healthcare

A layer that sits alongside care

We do not provide medical treatment or present recreational activities as a replacement for clinical mental-health care. We create a psychosocial and developmental layer that sits alongside existing healthcare.

By partnering with hospitals, clinics, families and communities, we bring support closer to where children already receive care.

Good intentions are not enough. Our pilots are designed to show what works, for whom and in what context, so we can keep improving.
